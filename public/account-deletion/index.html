WineCellar Account Deletion

Use this page to request deletion of your WineCellar account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

Email us from the email address linked to your WineCellar account.

Send to: winecellarcustomerservice@gmail.com

Subject: Account deletion request

Include: your full name, account email, phone number, and any recent order number if available.

Data that may be deleted

  • Customer account profile and login access.
  • Saved cart, wishlist, and account preferences.
  • Optional marketing preferences and non-essential profile details.

Data that may be retained

Some records may need to be retained for legal, tax, accounting, licensing, payment, fraud prevention, refund, delivery, or dispute reasons.

  • Completed order, payment, invoice, refund, and tax records may be retained for up to 6 years, or longer if legally required.
  • Open order, pickup, delivery, UPS tracking, refund, or cancellation records may be retained until the process is complete.
  • Security and fraud-prevention records may be retained where necessary to protect customers and payment systems.

Response time

We aim to respond as soon as possible. Where UK data protection law applies, we normally respond within one month after verifying your identity.

For more details, read our Privacy Policy.
